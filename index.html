HAVE QUESTIONS?

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you need to know about joining NEUROFIT, our training floor, and memberships.

What membership should I choose?
If you have experience training independently and want unrestricted 24/7 access to world-class iron equipment, the Basic tier is ideal. If you want structured group conditioning, regular body composition testing, and access to our contrast recovery plunge, choose Performance (most popular). For dedicated 1-on-1 private coaching, select Elite.
Do you offer personal training?
Yes. All our personal trainers hold internationally recognized strength and conditioning certifications (CSCS, NASM, FRC). You can book 1-on-1 blocks or enroll in our Elite membership plan.
Is the gym open 24/7?
Yes, 365 days a year. Members receive a secure biometric / RFID digital key on their smartphone to unlock the doors and security gates at any hour.
Do beginners need prior lifting experience?
No prior experience is necessary! Every new member receives a complimentary 45-minute orientation and movement screen with one of our coaches to learn proper form safely.
Can I cancel or freeze my membership?
Yes. We do not lock members into predatory contracts. Monthly memberships can be cancelled or frozen for up to 60 days with just a 7-day advance notice via our member portal.
Do you offer nutrition guidance?
Yes. Our Performance and Elite memberships include custom macro target calculations, meal structuring guidance, and supplement protocols tailored to your training demands.
